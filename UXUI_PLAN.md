# XYLO — UI/UX Plan for GitHub Pages
> Premium on-device document intelligence with AI-powered chat

## 📋 Executive Summary

XYLO is the **international version** of UNAWAIN. Same powerful on-device AI, rebranded for global markets. The killer differentiator: **document chat** — scan any document, get an AI summary, then ask questions about it. Everything runs locally on your device.

**Key Positioning:**
- Premium upfront paid app (no freemium)
- English-first (extensible to other languages)
- Document chat as hero feature
- 100% private & offline
- iPhone 15 Pro+ (6 GB RAM minimum)

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Premium & Modern**: Clean, minimal design that justifies upfront purchase
2. **Privacy-First**: Everything on-device, zero data collection
3. **Intelligence-Focused**: AI chat as the main story, not just document scanning
4. **Accessible**: Global audience, not culturally specific
5. **Trust & Transparency**: Clear about capabilities, requirements, and limitations

### Visual Language: "Prism"

- **Violet-Gold Gradient Palette** — Inspired by light refraction, representing insight and clarity
- **Glass Morphism** — Frosted blur cards conveying transparency and premium feel
- **Geometric Patterns** — Subtle diagonal weave texture (non-cultural)
- **Waveform Metaphor** — Not literal waveforms, but flowing gradient bars suggesting AI processing

**Differentiation from UNAWAIN:**
- UNAWAIN = Filipino cultural identity (Solihiya, Baybayin, Sampaguita)
- XYLO = International, minimal, modern, premium

### 13. FAQ Section

**Q: How does document chat work offline?**
A: XYLO downloads an AI model (Gemma 4 Edge) to your device during setup. All analysis and chat happens locally using your phone's Neural Engine. No internet needed after initial setup.

**Q: What types of documents are supported?**
A: PDF uploads, camera scans, mixed text/image documents. Works best with text-heavy documents up to 20 pages.

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**
A: XYLO uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to ensure answers cite actual document content. It will tell you if an answer isn't in the document.

**Q: Why is it only for iPhone 15 Pro+?**
A: Document chat requires significant on-device AI processing. Older devices don't have enough memory (6 GB minimum) to run the models smoothly.

**Q: Can I use it in other languages?**
A: Currently optimized for English documents. Additional language support planned for future updates.

**Q: Is my data really private?**
A: Yes. Zero network communication after model download. You can verify with iOS network monitoring tools. Delete the app, and all data is gone.
