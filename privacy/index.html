Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

Our Commitment

XYLO is built with privacy as a core principle. We believe your documents are yours alone.

Data Collection: None

XYLO does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data to our servers or third parties. Period.

How XYLO Works

  • On-Device Processing: All document analysis, AI processing, and chat conversations happen entirely on your device using local AI models.
  • No Cloud Communication: After the initial AI model download, XYLO operates completely offline. You can verify this with iOS network monitoring tools.
  • No Analytics: We don't use analytics services, tracking pixels, or any third-party data collection tools.
  • No User Accounts: XYLO doesn't require account creation, login, or any personal information.

Data Storage

All documents and analysis results are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage (SwiftData). This data is:

  • Protected by your device's encryption
  • Never backed up to iCloud (by design)
  • Completely deleted when you uninstall the app

App Permissions

XYLO requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: Only used for document scanning. Photos are processed locally and not saved to your photo library unless you explicitly save them.
  • Storage: To save analysis results on your device.

AI Model Download

On first launch, XYLO downloads an AI model (Gemma 4 Edge, 3.5-5.4 GB) from our servers. This is the only network communication the app performs. The download is secured over HTTPS and contains no user data.

Third-Party Services

XYLO does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools.

Children's Privacy

XYLO does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update it here and change the "Last Updated" date. Since XYLO doesn't collect data, changes will only clarify our practices, not introduce new data collection.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at privacy@xyloapp.com
